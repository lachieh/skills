# Document modes

Choose the mode from the reader's task. Split or link sections when a document
needs more than one mode. Do not force unlike content into one structure.

## Tutorial

Teach by guiding the reader through a complete result. State what they will
build, produce visible progress early, give expected output, and keep background
explanation short enough that it does not interrupt the work.

## How-to

Help a competent reader accomplish a specific goal. Give the required actions,
conditions, and decision points. Link background material instead of teaching it
inside the procedure.

## Reference

Provide facts for lookup. Mirror the structure of the code, command, API, or
configuration. State options, defaults, limits, errors, and compatibility. Keep
recommendations and narrative elsewhere.

## Explanation

Help the reader understand one bounded topic. Explain context, constraints,
history, tradeoffs, and alternatives around a real question. State the chosen
view when the evidence supports one.

Tutorials and how-to guides serve action. Reference and explanation serve
understanding. Tutorials and explanations support learning. How-to guides and
reference support work already in progress.
