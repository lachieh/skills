# Candidate prompt

Produce one complete version of the assigned artifact from the supplied
grounding, task, acceptance criteria, and isolated output path.

Work independently. Do not inspect other candidates. Choose one coherent design
and carry it through the artifact rather than listing options.

Return:

- The artifact at the assigned path.
- A short rationale naming the central choices.
- Alternatives considered and why they were rejected.
- Verification performed and remaining uncertainty.

The rationale is design context, not proof. The boss and an independent verifier
will inspect the artifact directly.
