# Unslop

Use this procedure for prose artifacts before review, and for the labels,
captions, helper text, and empty states a code change adds. Keep routine
conversation clean while drafting rather than loading the full review catalog on
every turn.

## Procedure

1. Identify the artifact, audience, purpose, intended voice, and the medium
   that will render it. Offer only formatting that medium shows.
2. List every style rule the human has named in this conversation, such as an
   author to follow or a paragraph length, and check each edit against the list.
   When editing the human's own writing, change what they asked to change and
   leave the rest of their words alone.
3. Preserve the original claims, evidence, and meaning.
4. Read `references/lachie-voice.md`, `references/word-choice.md`,
   `references/punctuation.md`, and `references/patterns.md`.
5. Rewrite for directness, specificity, concrete actors, and the project's real
   terminology.
6. Remove every applicable pattern finding and apply the word-choice and
   punctuation rules.
7. Verify links, symbols, paths, counts, quotations, and attributions against the
   current artifact.
8. Read the result once for awkward or generated phrasing. Stop when each
   sentence earns its place.

## Completion

The revision preserves meaning, introduces no unsupported claim, uses real
project terminology, fits its audience, and has no applicable findings from the
references.
