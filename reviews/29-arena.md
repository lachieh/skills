# Review 29: Arena

Status: approved and implemented

Source: `tmp/pstack/skills/arena/SKILL.md`

Implementation: `skills/arena/SKILL.md`

## Verdict

Keep Arena as the procedure that operationalizes Exhaust the Design Space for
one artifact.

## Approved changes

- Give every candidate the same grounding, task, and acceptance criteria.
- Isolate all candidate writes.
- Use two or three distinct providers or design directions.
- Resolve models through `configure-models`.
- Use an independent cross-provider judge.
- Have the boss inspect every candidate and own selection.
- Delegate bounded integration after the boss selects the base and ideas.
- Verify the synthesized artifact independently.
- Preserve a decision record with selections, integrations, rejections,
  dropouts, and evidence.

## Boundary

Arena compares several versions of one artifact and produces one synthesized
result. Swarm owns independent slices and coverage, not same-artifact races.
