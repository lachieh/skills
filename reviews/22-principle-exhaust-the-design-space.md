retain applicability and comparison guidance in the same document.

## Approved principle

When a consequential design has multiple viable shapes, build two or three
meaningfully distinct prototypes or sketches and compare them side by side
before committing. A variation of the first idea does not count as another
design.

## Supporting guidance

- Apply it to novel interactions, architecture without precedent, product
  decisions that depend on feel, and empirically answerable design questions.
- Make prototypes cheap to discard and complete enough to answer the question.
- Compare against user goals, intended outcomes, constraints, failure modes,
  cost, and experience.
- Leave mechanical work with an established pattern out of design exploration.
- Let architecture, arena, and prototype procedures operationalize the rule.
