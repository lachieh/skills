The upstream testing leaf's assertion taxonomy is inaccurate. For example,
`toBeDefined`, `toBeTruthy`, and `toEqual([])` do not pass on `undefined`.
An absent write can also be the safety contract a test must protect. The local
principle therefore asks which defect an assertion detects instead of banning
matchers or requiring a literal value for every kind of test.

history, repetition, figurative warnings, and long examples are already absent.
Keep the compact local contracts instead of replacing them with upstream prose.
