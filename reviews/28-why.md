## Verdict

Keep the historical investigation procedure with Targeted and Archaeology
branches that share one evidence model and synthesis path.

## Branch contract

- Targeted is the default for a specific rationale question.
- Archaeology applies to broad history, postmortems, disputed rationale,
  consequential thresholds, and explicit full-coverage requests.
- Both anchor in code, delegate source-specific research, classify evidence,
  verify citations, and expose contradictions and gaps.

## Approved changes

- Discover evidence capabilities from the host.
- Use configured exploration and synthesis roles.
- Keep investigators read-only while granting authorized evidence access.
- Treat empty searches as coverage information rather than proof of absence.
- Search only relevant and linked categories in Targeted mode.
- Keep broad category coverage in Archaeology mode.
- Let the boss verify citations and own the final account.
