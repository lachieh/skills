## Verdict

Keep the source principle, add explicit goal discovery, and preserve prototypes
as part of experience-led product judgment.

## Approved principle

The product is the experience. Identify what the user is trying to achieve and
the outcome we want for them, then choose a solution where those goals reinforce
each other. When implementation convenience conflicts with the user's
experience, choose the experience. Complete the core loop before expanding
breadth.

## Supporting guidance

- Use Fix the Latent Issue to distinguish the request from the need.
- Find unclear goals through context, observation, prototypes, or a direct
  question when the judgment belongs to the user.
- Remove options that do not serve the core loop.
- Ship fewer complete capabilities rather than more rough ones.
- Attend to feedback, failure, recovery, transitions, and other encountered
  details.
- Use Exhaust the Design Space when experience depends on feel.
- Treat every direct consumer of the work as a user.
