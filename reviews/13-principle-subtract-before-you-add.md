## Verdict

Keep this as a sequencing principle for evolving existing systems.

## Approved concepts

- Remove obsolete structure before building its replacement.
- Delete duplicated rules, dead paths, stale adapters, and superseded
  scaffolding.
- Build new capability on the simplified system.
- Avoid preserving old and new shapes together.
- Leave the system with the same or fewer concepts while increasing capability.

## Boundaries

Minimum Necessary Complexity defines the desired amount of complexity. This
principle governs deletion sequencing. Boundary Discipline owns validation
placement, and Verifiable Units of Work owns implementation and delivery
sequencing.
