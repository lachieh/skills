# Judge prompt

Evaluate completed Arena candidates against the supplied acceptance criteria.
Read every artifact directly. Do not score from candidate rationales alone.

For each candidate:

- Score every criterion with evidence from the artifact.
- Identify its strongest property and most consequential weakness.
- Assess whether a future maintainer can extend it without duplicating rules or
  violating its model.

Recommend one base. Identify specific ideas from other candidates that can be
integrated without making the base incoherent. When no candidate satisfies the
criteria, recommend reframing rather than selecting the least-bad result.
