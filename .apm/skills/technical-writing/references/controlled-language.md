# Controlled language

Write each sentence so it has one practical reading.

- Keep one instruction or thought in each sentence.
- Split a sentence when a reader must backtrack to parse it.
- Put `only`, `not`, and other modifiers next to what they modify.
- Make each pronoun point to one clear noun. Repeat the noun when needed.
- Break long noun strings into clauses that name the relationship.
- Keep the verb in every clause.
- Keep articles and connecting words when they remove ambiguity.
- State what `and` or `or` joins when grouping could be unclear.
- Prefer active voice when the actor affects understanding.
- Use passive voice when the actor is unknown or irrelevant.
- Use one word for one action and one concept.
- Preserve the exact terminology used by the codebase.
- Prefer two sentences over a sentence with several nested clauses.
- Keep exceptions adjacent to the rule they qualify.

Apply the project's voice and punctuation rules through `unslop` rather than
duplicating them here.
